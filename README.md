## What is NextSteer?

NextSteer gives mouse, gamepad and keyboard players of **Assetto Corsa** what a steering wheel would.
It runs inside the game through **Custom Shaders Patch (CSP)** and works on every car.

This is the **free demo**: try it, have fun, and tell us what you think. It is not the full version —
the full NextSteer has more systems and more settings, and it keeps growing with every update.

## Highlights

- 🖱️ **Mouse** — smooth, precise steering, with force feedback and auto counter-steer
- 🎮 **Gamepad** — Smart Steering, fitted to each car while you drive
- ⌨️ **Keyboard** — analog-like steering from digital keys
- 🎛️ **Presets** — ready-made setups, and codes to share your own in any chat
- 📏 **Steering Bar**: your input and the wheel on screen, and again when you watch a replay
- 🌍 **7 languages** — English, Português, Español, Français, Italiano, Русский, 日本語

> **About the built-in presets:** they are tuned to my own taste, so they may not feel right to you.
> Try them, then adjust the settings and save your own preset — that is how NextSteer feels best.

## Requirements

- **Assetto Corsa** (Steam)
- **Custom Shaders Patch 0.2.11 or newer**
- **Content Manager** (to install it by drag and drop)

## Install

1. **Download** `NextSteer-Demo-<version>.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/therealmrtaylor/NextSteer-Demo/releases/latest). Do not unzip it.
2. **Drag the zip into Content Manager**, then click **Install** (in Downloads).
3. In Content Manager:
   - **Controls → Input method: Gamepad**
   - **Custom Shaders Patch → Gamepad FX:** turn it on, choose **NextSteer Demo** and allow its script
4. **Start a session.** Open **NextSteer Demo** from CSP's app bar, pick your language and press **Start**.

> Gamepad FX not on? NextSteer notices it in game and offers to turn itself on.

## Update

Drag the new zip into Content Manager and install it over the old one. Your settings are kept.

## Uninstall

Delete these two folders inside your Assetto Corsa folder:

```
apps/lua/NextSteerDemo
extension/lua/joypad-assist/NextSteerDemo
```

## Found a bug?

Tell us on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/k7j4z3MPSU) and attach the `errors.log` file from
`apps/lua/NextSteerDemo/` (and `extension/lua/joypad-assist/NextSteerDemo/`, if there is one).
